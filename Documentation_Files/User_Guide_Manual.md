## 1. Introduction

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to use the features of the **AlumNet** system. It is intended for **Public Visitors / Guests**, **Cecilian Alumni**, **Corporate Recruiters / Industry Partners**, and **Institutional Administrators** (Alumni Affairs Office & Registrar), helping them navigate, manage, and operate the system efficiently.

### 1.1 Problems Addressed by the System
* **Graduate Career Blindspots**: When students graduate, institutions traditionally lose contact and have no systematic mechanism for tracking where graduates are employed, their industry sector, or whether their current employment aligns with their academic program.
* **Chaotic and Fragmented Event Communication**: During alumni homecomings, reunions, and conferences, organizers struggle to gather attendees across scattered, informal messaging channels (e.g., fragmented Facebook Messenger threads or WhatsApp groups), resulting in missed updates, duplicated logistics, and low engagement.
* **Vulnerable Identity Verification**: Physical IDs and unverified digital lists are prone to falsification or duplicate records without real-time cross-referencing against the registrar masterlist.

### 1.2 The system has been developed to:
* **Eliminate Graduate Career Blindspots**: Track alumni post-graduation employment status, industry sectors, and program alignment through a centralized digital tracer module adhering to Commission on Higher Education (CHED) standards.
* **Streamline Event Coordination & Attendance**: Automate RSVP ticketing with encrypted digital passes and **automatically enroll attendees into the official Event Group Chat room** immediately upon reservation to eliminate communication chaos.
* **Modernize Institutional Identity**: Provide verified graduates with permanent, interactive 3D Digital Alumni Cards embedded with dynamic cryptographic QR verification.
* **Foster Direct Career Pathways**: Host verified corporate job vacancies, one-click PDF resume applications, and real-time applicant tracking system (ATS) pipelines.
* **Protect Institutional Data Integrity**: Cross-match alumni claims against official registrar masterlist records before granting access.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 System Requirements
* **Operating Systems**: Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, Linux (Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora), Android 9.0 and above, iOS 14.0 and above.
* **Web Browsers**: Google Chrome (v100+), Mozilla Firefox (v100+), Apple Safari (v14+), Microsoft Edge (latest versions).
* **Hardware Requirements**:
  * *Desktop / Laptop*: 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended), dual-core 2.0 GHz processor or higher, screen resolution 1366×768 or higher.
  * *Mobile Device*: Smartphone or tablet with camera support (for contactless QR ticket and digital ID scanning) and touch interface.
* **Network & Connectivity**: Stable Wi-Fi or cellular data connection (minimum 2 Mbps download/upload speed).

### 2.2 Accessing the System
1. Open a supported web browser or launch the installed progressive web app (PWA) on your mobile device.
2. Enter the official system URL: `https://alumnet.stcecilia.edu.ph` or scan the official QR code provided by the St. Cecilia’s College Alumni Affairs Office.
3. Access Modes:
   * **Public Visitor / Guest**: No login required. Can view institutional announcements, public event calendars, and general college tracer statistics.
   * **Alumni Member**: Log in using your verified student number or email address and password.
   * **Corporate Recruiter**: Log in using approved industry partner credentials.
   * **System / School Administrator**: Log in using institutional administrative credentials.

## 3. User Roles and Permissions

### 3.1 Public Users / Campus Visitors
* View public campus bulletins, upcoming homecoming schedules, and general event announcements.
* Browse aggregated institutional graduate tracer statistics and employment rates.
* Access the Alumni Registration Portal to begin identity verification.
* *Restrictions*: Cannot view private alumni directory profiles, contact numbers, internal event group chats, or corporate applicant pools.

### 3.2 Cecilian Alumni
* Claim and verify alumni identity against registrar student records.
* Update employment records, current job titles, company affiliations, and work locations in the Graduate Tracer.
* View, tilt, and present the interactive 3D Digital Alumni ID Card with encrypted QR verification.
* Browse upcoming alumni reunions, reserve digital tickets, and **automatically enter dedicated Event Group Chats upon RSVP**.
* Search verified corporate vacancies, submit digital PDF applications, and monitor hiring pipeline stages.
* Direct message fellow alumni and participate in batch-specific discussion circles.

### 3.3 Corporate Recruiters / Industry Partners
* Manage verified corporate profiles, contact information, and institutional hiring accreditation.
* Post active career opportunities, internships, and entry-level to senior positions.
* Filter candidates based on academic degrees, graduation year, and technical skills.
* Review candidate profiles, download submitted PDF resumes, and update recruitment pipeline statuses.

### 3.4 Institutional Administrators (Alumni Affairs Office & Registrar)
* Full access to institutional graduate tracer analytics, CHED alignment KPIs, and demographic charts.
* Resolve unverified alumni registration claims, review uploaded credentials, and match records against the official student database.
* Create, publish, and supervise alumni events, ticketing tiers, and monitor live contactless QR check-in desks.
* Moderate group chat communities, broadcast campus-wide notices, and export audit-ready tracer reports.
* Provision university accounts with role-based access control (RBAC) and quota enforcement.

## 4. Step-by-Step Alumni User Instructions

### 4.1 Alumni Onboarding & Registrar Verification
1. Navigate to the login screen and click **"Register as Alumni"**.
2. Enter your assigned **Student ID Number** (e.g., `2022-0412`), **Full Legal Name**, **Degree Program**, and **Year of Graduation**.
3. The system executes automated cross-matching against the St. Cecilia's registrar database:
   * *Match Found*: Set your account password and contact details to complete instant onboarding.
   * *Discrepancy / Legacy Record*: Upload supporting identification (e.g., Diploma copy, Transcript of Records, or valid government ID) for manual registrar triage review.
4. Log into your dashboard to access your newly generated **3D Digital Alumni ID**.

### 4.2 Updating Employment & Graduate Tracer Status
1. From the top navigation bar, select **"Tracer / My Career"**.
2. Click **"Update Employment Profile"**.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   * *Current Employment Status* (Employed Full-time, Employed Part-time, Self-Employed / Entrepreneur, Actively Seeking, Pursuing Graduate Studies).
   * *Job Title & Organization / Employer Name*.
   * *Employment Sector & Work Location* (Local Cebu, Regional Philippines, Overseas OFW, or Fully Remote).
   * *Course Alignment* (Indicate whether your current position directly utilizes your college degree training).
   * *Salary Range Tier* (Confidential for institutional statistical reporting).
4. Click **"Save Changes"**. The institutional tracer analytics will automatically update in real-time.

### 4.3 Event Registration & Automatic Group Chat Access
1. Navigate to the **"Events"** section on the main navigation menu.
2. Select an active event (e.g., *Grand Alumni Homecoming 2026*, *Cecilian Tech Summit*).
3. Review event specifics: Date, Time, Venue, and Available Passes.
4. Click **"Reserve Seat / Get Pass"**.
5. Once confirmed:
   * A digital ticket pass containing a dynamic cryptographic QR code will be generated in your **"My Passes"** wallet.
   * The system **automatically enrolls your account into the official Event Group Chat**, granting immediate real-time messaging access to coordinate logistics, travel, and reunions with fellow attendees.

### 4.4 Utilizing the 3D Digital Alumni ID Card
1. Click the **"Digital ID"** icon on the navigation bar.
2. Hover or drag across the card to rotate it in 3D space, showing the official St. Cecilia's seal, hologram band, and graduate verification badge.
3. Click the card to flip it over to reveal the **Encrypted QR Code**.
4. Present this code at campus security gates or event reception checkpoints for immediate contactless validation.

### 4.5 Corporate Career Hub & Job Applications
1. Select **"Jobs & Careers"** from the menu.
2. Filter active job listings by department, position level, or job type (On-site, Hybrid, Remote).
3. Click on a listing to review job descriptions, minimum qualifications, and hiring employer profiles.
4. Click **"Quick Apply"** to submit your linked AlumNet profile and attached PDF resume.
5. Track application progress under the **"Applied Jobs"** tab (*Submitted*, *Under Review*, *Interview Scheduled*, *Decision*).

## 5. Corporate Recruiter Operating Procedures

### 5.1 Employer Portal Setup & Verification
1. Register an organization account under **"Employer Partner Registration"**.
2. Submit corporate credentials, business registration number, and official representative contact details.
3. Once approved by the Alumni Affairs Office, access the **Employer Management Workspace**.

### 5.2 Posting Career Vacancies
1. From the Employer Workspace, navigate to **"Post a Vacancy"**.
2. Specify the job title, academic degree preference, employment classification, salary bracket, and job description.
3. Click **"Publish Opportunity"**. The vacancy is instantly distributed to eligible Cecilian graduates.

### 5.3 Managing Candidate Applications
1. Open the **"Candidate Pipeline"** view.
2. Click on an applicant's dossier to inspect their degree credentials, graduation batch, and preview their submitted PDF resume.
3. Update the candidate's hiring stage (*Shortlisted*, *Interview Call*, *Hired*, or *Archived*). The applicant receives an automated in-app status notification.

## 6. Administrator & Registrar Operating Procedures

### 6.1 Graduate Tracer Analytics & Accreditation Dashboards
1. Log into the Administrator Workspace and select **"Graduate Tracer"**.
2. Review real-time KPIs: Overall Employment Rate, Program-to-Job Alignment Ratio, Top Hiring Companies, and Regional vs. Overseas distribution.
3. Apply filters by Academic Department (e.g., College of Computer Studies, Business Administration, Education) and Batch Year.
4. Click **"Export Audit Report"** to generate CHED-compliant summaries.

### 6.2 Contactless QR Event Check-In Desk
1. Under **"Events Management"**, select the ongoing event and click **"Launch QR Ticket Scanner"**.
2. Point the device camera or connected optical scanner at the arriving alumnus's digital ticket pass.
3. The system decrypts the ticket cryptographic signature:
   * *Valid Pass*: Displays attendee name, batch, seat number, and logs check-in timestamp with an audible confirmation chime.
   * *Duplicate / Invalid*: Flags a red alert if the pass has already been scanned, preventing unauthorized entry.
4. Check-in records synchronize live with the downloadable attendance audit report.

### 6.3 Event Group Chat Moderation
1. Access the **"Community & Channels"** panel.
2. Supervise event group chat threads, pin administrative circulars, broadcast venue announcements, and moderate content adhering to institutional guidelines.

### 6.4 Registrar Masterlist Triage Queue
1. Open the **"Registrar Triage"** tab.
2. Inspect pending student claims flagged for discrepancies.
3. Cross-reference uploaded TOR / Diploma documents against the digitized masterlist.
4. Click **"Verify & Grant Alumni Status"** or **"Reject Claim with Feedback"**.

### 6.5 Provisioning Accounts & Quota Policies
1. In the Admin Workspace, click **"Add Member"**.
2. Fill in the required fields: Full Legal Name, Institutional Email Address, System Role, and Default Password.
3. The system enforces validation rules:
   * Flags missing required fields with inline alerts.
   * Prevents duplicate email registrations.
   * Enforces strict Institutional Moderator quotas (maximum 3 active accounts).
4. Click **"Create Member Account"**. Upon success, the system presents the verified confirmation screen displaying the assigned role and provisions the account in Cloud Firestore.

## 7. Troubleshooting & Support

| Issue / Error Message | Probable Cause | Recommended Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Student ID Not Found in Masterlist"** | Typographical error in student ID or student record has not yet been digitized by the registrar. | Verify the correct format (e.g., `2022-0412`). If accurate, click "Submit Manual Verification" and attach a photo of your Diploma or TOR for administrator approval. |
| **"Invalid or Duplicate QR Pass"** at check-in | Pass has already been registered as entered, or ticket screen brightness is too low. | Increase mobile display brightness. Verify the attendee identity against the manual attendee list in the admin search bar. |
| **"Group Chat Access Restricted"** | Event reservation was cancelled or event has passed its active archival window. | Verify your ticket status under "My Passes". If your ticket is active, click "Re-sync Event Channels" on the settings menu. |
| **"Moderator Quota Limit Reached"** | System already has 3 active institutional moderator accounts. | Deactivate an existing moderator profile or assign an alternative administrative role. |
| **3D Digital ID fails to render smoothly** | Web browser hardware acceleration is disabled or device GPU memory is constrained. | Enable hardware acceleration in browser settings or toggle the "2D Static View" switch on the Digital ID screen. |
| **Resume Upload Failed** | File exceeds the 10 MB limit or is in an unsupported format. | Ensure the resume document is formatted as a PDF file and does not exceed 10 MB in file size. |
