Core Creative Concept & Narrative Arc

The Problem: When students graduate, colleges lose track of where they are working, creating an accreditation blindspot. Furthermore, when organizing campus events, gathering attendees into unified group chats is notoriously chaotic.

The Solution: AlumNet provides instant registrar verification, live employment tracer tracking, 3D digital alumni ID cards, and automatic event group chat enrollment upon RSVP, culminating in the collegiate tagline: "Where Legacy Lives On."
