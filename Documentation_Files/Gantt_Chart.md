## Agile Sprint Schedule Breakdown

1. **Sprint 0: Project Inception & Agile Architecture Setup** (6/15/2026 – 6/26/2026)
   - Architectural scoping, user story backlog grooming, and UI/UX design wireframes.
2. **Sprint 1: User Registration & Authentication** (6/29/2026 – 7/17/2026)
   - Firebase Auth integration, institutional registration forms, and app preferences.
3. **Sprint 2: Dashboard Overview & Profile Engine** (7/20/2026 – 8/7/2026)
   - Profile loading, real-time analytics counter, quick action cards, and verification banners.
4. **Sprint 3: Content Sections & Campus Bulletins** (8/10/2026 – 8/28/2026)
   - Event carousels, tap-to-view announcements, opportunities feed, and network recommendations.
5. **Sprint 4: Networking Features & Connection Protocol** (8/31/2026 – 9/18/2026)
   - Friends management, connection request validation, and alumni discovery.
6. **Sprint 5: Direct Messaging & Real-Time Communication** (9/21/2026 – 10/2/2026)
   - 1-on-1 direct peer chats, cohort group chats, and Web Push notifications.
7. **Sprint 6: Career Opportunities & Employer ATS Portal** (10/5/2026 – 10/16/2026)
   - Corporate employer vetting, job posting lifecycle, and applicant tracking pipeline.
8. **Sprint 7: Registrar Verification & Governance** (10/19/2026 – 10/30/2026)
   - Masterlist CSV importer, conflict arbitration workspace, and security audit log ledger.
9. **Sprint 8: Agile Quality Assurance, UAT & Production Launch** (11/2/2026 – 11/7/2026)
   - Comprehensive test suite validation, security hardening, and final production release.
