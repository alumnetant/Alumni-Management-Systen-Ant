## Executive Summary & Core Narrative Arc

The 60-second showcase addresses the critical post-graduation challenges experienced by both Cecilian graduates and the college administration:
1. **The Blindspot**: Once students graduate, the college loses track of where they are working, creating an accreditation tracer gap.
2. **The Event Coordination Chaos**: When campus reunions and homecomings occur, organizing attendees into unified group chats is notoriously difficult and scattered across fragmented messaging apps.
3. **The Solution (AlumNet)**: Seamless automated registrar verification, real-time employment status tracking, 3D digital alumni passes with dynamic QR codes, accredited career placement, and **automatic enrollment into the official event group chat upon RSVP**.
4. **The Closing**: Uniting alumni worldwide with the timeless institutional tagline: *"Where Legacy Lives On."*

## Technical Specifications & Production Guidelines

1. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio**:
   - Master Presentation: 1920x1080 Full HD (16:9 Landscape) for web landing page, auditorium displays, and YouTube.
   - Mobile Social Cut: 1080x1920 (9:16 Portrait) for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Stories.
2. **Color Palette Tokens**:
   - Deep Cecilian Crimson: `#8B181B` (Dominant brand accent)
   - Heritage Warm Canvas: `#FAF9F6` (Card background & light posture)
   - Institutional Gold: `#D97706` (Accents, badges, seals)
   - Emerald Verification: `#059669` (Registrar match indicator)
   - Slate Neutral: `#1C1917` (Deep typographic contrast)
3. **Typography**:
   - Headers & Display: Inter Display / Outfit (Tight tracking `-0.02em`, Bold to Extrabold)
   - Subtitles & Captions: Inter (Crisp legibility at 18px on video)
   - Institutional Tagline: Editorial serif italic pairing for *"Where Legacy Lives On"*
4. **Audio Mix Guidelines**:
   - Voiceover Level: Normalized at -14 LUFS (peaking at -1.5 dB).
   - Background Music: Sits at -24 dB during voiceover, auto-ducking by 6 dB during speech pauses.
